GRIHA — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

1. Introduction

GRIHA ("we", "us", "our") operates a mobile application that helps users in Mumbai discover real estate price information, browse and post property and project listings, and connect directly with property owners, agents, and builders. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By using GRIHA, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Name, phone number, email address: Collected when you create your profile (via Google Sign-In and profile completion).
  • Role: (Individual, Agent, or Builder) used to determine listing permissions.
  • Company name and RERA number: For users posting builder projects.
  • Property/project details: Listing type, price, size, BHK, location, amenities, possession date, and similar details you enter when posting a listing.
  • Photos and documents: Property/project photos and project brochures you upload.
  • Requirement/callback details: Submitted via "Post Your Requirement" or "Request a Callback".

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Location: Precise or approximate device location, used to center the map and locality search around you.
  • Device/push notification token: Used to deliver push notifications about leads, payments, and listing status.
  • App activity: Your saved properties, submitted listings, leads, and in-app notifications.

2.3 Payment information

When you pay for a listing fee or a lead batch, payment is processed by Razorpay. GRIHA does not receive or store your card, UPI, or bank details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate, moderate, and improve the GRIHA app, process payments, deliver notifications, and connect users directly with property owners or builders. We do not sell your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

4. How Information Is Shared

When you post a listing or express interest in a project, your contact details (name, phone number, email) are shared with interested parties or project developers as part of our direct-contact model. Limited data is shared with service providers (Google, Firebase, Razorpay) strictly to operate app features.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on servers located in Mumbai, India. We implement standard industry security practices (HTTPS encryption) to protect your personal information.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and listing information as long as your account is active. You can request full data deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data.

To Delete Your Account: You can delete your account directly inside the GRIHA app settings under Profile > Delete Account, or submit a request via email to echostacklab@gmail.com.
